A highlighted cascade target waits its turn (#171)

Three filled triangles revealed by a cascade of A.Polygon.outlineAndFill. The draw guard is !visible && !shouldHighlight && emphasisAmount <= 0 — three terms that all have to agree for a pending target to stay off the canvas. The deferred reveal (#83) holds visible = false and #104 defers the emphasis, but shouldHighlight was still set at run start. So when the targets were also in the slide's highlighted set, all three painted the moment the slide opened — face fill and label at full strength — then each blanked and redrew when its turn came. That is II.1's three rectangles, and 70 cascades across 29 pages have the shape.

Press p (or the play control), then step with →:

  1. Highlighted cascade — the case that was wrong. Before the fix: all three faces appear at once, then flicker one by one. After: each appears only as its own outline begins, exactly like slide 3.
  2. Everything hidden again.
  3. Plain cascade — the same three, not highlighted. This always worked; it is the reference for what slide 1 should look like.
