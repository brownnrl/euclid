Drag any free point (A, B, or C) — the scene should rotate/scale smoothly around the equilateral-triangle apex D (green) which is the pivot. In the Java applet (windows 1 & 2 of the three-way harness) the rotation is visually stable. In the TS port before the fix for issue #41, dragging A even a small amount balloons the outer circle GKL and the radius circle CGH disproportionately — the points labeled L, E, F, G, H, K are all point;last;... references to existing line endpoints, and a missing preexists flag caused them to be moved twice in the rotation loop.
