Slider gliding to a derived point — A.Point.slide { to: "E" } (#122)

P is a lineSlider on CD; E is the derived intersection of AB and CD. Press p to present, → to walk: P hunts along the line, then slides to the element E — resolved + projected at runtime, so it lands exactly on the intersection. The key test: drag A, B, C, or D, then present again — E moves, and P still lands on it (no hand-computed parameter to go stale). This is the I.20 Heron "search → land on E" motion.
