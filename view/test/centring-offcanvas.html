Off-canvas anchors do not define the centring frame (#193)

II.6's shape, as the converter emits it: two slider anchors A0 and A3 at x = ±1000 with the letter suppressed but the default vertex dot still on (A0;point;free;-1000,40;0). They paint, so the #138 ink test keeps them; at under three canvases out they are not #162 outliers either. So the maximized centring frame ran x ∈ [−1000, 1000] and the figure's centre was x = 0. A wide desktop canvas hides a 185 px offset; a 412 px phone puts the figure in the right half.

Now an element lying entirely outside the logical canvas box is left out of the frame, and named in centringOutliers. One that spills past an edge but reaches the canvas is still measured in full. Press m or p: the figure should sit in the middle. Narrow the window to phone width to see the case that was reported.
