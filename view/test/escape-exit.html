Escape leaves the maximized view (#139)

Escape peels off one layer of takeover per press.

  1. Click maximize (top-right of the canvas), then press Esc — the canvas returns to its inline size and resets. Before #139 nothing happened here and you had to find the minimize control or press m.
  2. Maximize again by clicking the button (so keyboard focus is on the button, not the canvas) and press Esc. It still exits — the handler is bound on document, unlike the canvas-scoped r/m/p shortcuts.
  3. Press p to present, walk a slide or two, drag point D, then press Esc once: you leave the walk but stay maximized with your drag intact (#115). Press Esc again: now you leave the maximized view and the figure resets.
  4. Press Esc on the inline canvas — nothing should happen (no reset, no jump).
