Highlighted / animating elements draw on top (#140)

Both canvases hold the same figure: the gold BD is declared before the black AC that crosses it. On the left, highlightOnTop is off (the pre-0.14.0 behaviour) — AC paints over the highlight and chops the gold at the crossing. On the right it is on (the default) — BD stays continuous.

Hover a {NAME} ref below to emphasise an element, and press p on either canvas to walk the slides: promotion holds for the whole animation and through the fade-out taper, so an element never drops back under its neighbours while it is still gold.

highlightOnTop: false — chopped highlightOnTop: true (default) — continuous
