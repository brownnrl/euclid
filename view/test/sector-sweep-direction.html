A sector sweep grows for either arm order (#172)

Three sectors, revealed by A.Sector.sweep. Left: a 90° sector with arms G,P,Q. Middle: the same sector with the arms swapped, G,Q,P. Right: a 180° semicircle — II.14's BHF.

The arc always goes one rotational direction from the first arm to the second — the original applet's contract (SectorElement.java adds 360° to a negative sweep) — so swapping the arms picks the other side: the middle sector draws the 270° complement, on purpose. That part is unchanged. What was wrong is how the animation got there: it scaled the signed angle, so for the arm order whose sign opposes the drawing direction the arc opened as a near-complete circle and shrank to its span. The semicircle showed it most plainly — a full circle unwinding to a half.

Press p, then →. Before the fix, the middle and right arcs open almost complete and close down. After, all three grow from their first arm. The table reads each sector's drawn sweep (arcSpan()) off the slate — note the 270°.
