maximized-view recenter (#107)

Click the maximize button (top-right of the canvas) — the figure slides to the centre of the enlarged canvas (translate only, no scaling) instead of sitting in the corner. Resize the window while maximized: it stays centred. Minimize: it returns to its authored spot. Press p to present: every slide stays centred; the case slide lays its copies without a jump. Drag a point while centred — it still tracks.
