maximized-view recenter (#107)

Click the maximize button (top-right of the canvas) — the figure slides to the centre of the enlarged canvas (translate only, no scaling) instead of sitting in the corner. Resize the window while maximized: it stays centred. Minimize: returns to the authored spot and resets. Press p to present: it centres once on enter, then each slide advance leaves the figure where it is — drag a point mid-walk and the next slide won't yank it back. The case slide lays its copies without a jump. Esc leaves the walk but stays maximized, keeping your manipulation; click minimize when you want the pristine figure back.
