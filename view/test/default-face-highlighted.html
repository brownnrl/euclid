The default opaque face on a promoted element (#182)

Both figures are a triangle ABC drawn over a black segment PQ that crosses it. The left triangle is declared with four fields (ABC;polygon;triangle;A,B,C), so the library fills in the default face: lighten(bgcolor), near-white and opaque. The right triangle says ;0;0;black;0, an explicit transparent face.

At rest they look the same, because the default face is the page colour and the segment is drawn on top of it. Now press p and step to slide 2, which highlights the triangle. #140 promotes a highlighted element to draw last, and on the left the opaque face paints over the segment: that is the I.41 / I.43 / I.44 / I.47 white-out. On the right the segment stays visible.

What #182 adds is the badge in the left canvas's corner, lit at load: the deck promotes an element nobody gave a face, and the library now says so instead of waiting for someone to notice a missing line. The right canvas is clean. The readouts below print geomlib.diagnostics() for each.
