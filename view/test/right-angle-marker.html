A right angle drawn as a square corner (#201)

III.31's figure, from the site: the angle in the semicircle. BAC is right (Thales), ABC in the greater segment is less than right, ADC in the less segment is greater. On the left the deck's current red marker, sector;angleMarker: a sector of about the same size as its neighbours, so "right" rides on colour alone. On the right the same element string with sector;angleMarkerRight: the conventional square corner. Drag A round the semicircle; the square stays square because the angle stays right. The radius (34 here) is the distance to the square's corner, so the mark has the same footprint as the sector it replaces and reads a little smaller, as a right-angle box conventionally does; clamp, override, palette, ring stepping and showAngles all behave as before.

Press p on the right canvas: A.Sector.sweep draws the two sides in turn, timed as a 90° sweep, and reverse: true starts from the other arm.
