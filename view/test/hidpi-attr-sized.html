An attribute-sized canvas on a HiDPI display (#173)

Both canvases are the same I.1 figure. The left one is laid out the way the site emits every figure: width/height attributes and no CSS size, so its box follows its bitmap. The right one is pinned by CSS.

This page only shows the bug on a display with devicePixelRatio other than 1 — a Windows laptop at 125–150% scaling, or any Mac. Before the fix the left figure rendered 1.5× too large, points could not be dragged, and each window resize grew the canvas again. After the fix both are identical: bitmap = client × dpr, and the box stays put. Resize the window a few times to confirm nothing compounds.
