Same-vertex marker rings on a derived vertex (#175)

Three angle markers share the vertex D: GDB at 72°, FDB at 90°, and ADG at 108°. Auto-nesting (#103) should put the smallest innermost, so the right angle sits between the other two.

On the left, D is the midpoint of AB — III.1's shape. The ring sort used to run before update() had placed the figure, so a midpoint (or intersection, or any derived point) was measured at garbage coordinates: the 90° marker took ring 0 and nested inside the 72° angle it contains. On the right, D is a free point, which always came out right — free points have coordinates from construction, which is how this hid.

The markers are declared with the right angle first, so declaration order can't accidentally produce the correct result. The tables read ringIndex and spanRadians() straight off the slate.
