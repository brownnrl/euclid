On-canvas diagnostic badge (#154)

geomlib skips what it cannot resolve and keeps rendering — the right call, but it used to mean the only evidence was a console line nobody has open. A canvas that reported anything now carries a small mark in its upper-left corner. Hover it for the tooltip.

  • Clean — no badge at all, and no extra DOM node is even created.
  • Warning — amber triangle. This deck animates GHOST, which no element or alias resolves to. The badge appears on load: decks are checked when the figure is built, so a dead target does not stay hidden until someone happens to advance to the slide that uses it.
  • Error — a red stop-sign octagon. Same figure, but something reported at error severity; error supersedes warning when both are present (this canvas reports one of each).

Things worth checking by hand: (1) the badge is visible without hovering — unlike the reset/maximize controls, which fade in on hover (#69); (2) press p to present, or maximize: the badge rides along to the viewport's upper-left and does not collide with the caption or nav; (3) press r (reset) — the badge clears, because reset is the deliberate "I've seen it" gesture; (4) the warning canvas and the clean canvas are independent: a warning on one must never badge the other.
